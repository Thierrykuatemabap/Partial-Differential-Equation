The associated codes here, illustrate the different stages of numerical resolution, from the formulation of the mathematical model to the computational implementation of discretization schemes. This section highlights the use of PDEs to represent phenomena that depend on both time and space, such as diffusion, transport, structured population dynamics, and certain biological or environmental processes. The provided scripts make it possible to construct approximate solutions, visualize their evolution, study the influence of model parameters, and compare the numerical results obtained. The main objective is to show how partial differential equations constitute a fundamental tool for understanding, simulating, and analysing complex systems arising from applied problems.
